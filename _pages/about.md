I am a 5th-year Ph.D. student in the Computer Science department at the University of Pittsburgh, working with Dr. Erin Walker in [FACET Lab](https://www.facetlab.pitt.edu/). My research sits at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction**, **Natural Language Processing**, and **Multimodal Machine Learning**. I design educational technologies that support learners in open-ended learning environments, with a particular focus on how learners interact with these systems across modalities, modeling their behaviors, and using these insights to design more effective and adaptive dialogue interactions. 

My work has focused on **multimodal hybrid dialogue systems**, exploring how Large Language Models (LLMs) can be combined with theory-driven approaches to create effective, adaptive interactions that support learner reflections in open-ended learning settings.

Most recently I was a Machine Learning Intern at Meta, Menlo Park, where I worked on user simulation for multi-turn benchmarking. Previously, I was a Software Engineer on the EC2 Enterprise team at Amazon Web Services, Seattle, where I built services and SDKs to help enterprise customers onboard new workloads on AWS.

I graduated with a Master's in Computer Science degree from the University of Southern California in 2019. At USC, I worked on several research projects as a research assistant with the Social Media Analytics lab and the Institute for Creative Technologies (ICT). Earlier, I completed my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science & Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT ISM) Dhanbad, which laid the foundation for my interests in computer science and research.
