Our paper titled **Towards Adaptive Culturally-Responsive Computing: Multimodal Sensing of Goals and Processes During Interactions With a Co-created Robot** got accepted to the ACM CHI conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems 2026. This paper integrates LLMs with rule-based systems to promote learner reflections during their interactions with a co-created robot.
