Our paper titled **Hybrid LLM-Embedded Dialogue Agents for Learner Reflection: Designing Responsive and Theory-Driven Interactions** got accepted to the ACM CHI conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems 2026. This paper integrates LLMs with rule-based systems to promote learner reflections during their interactions with a co-created robot.
